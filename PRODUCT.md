# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Fans and listeners of RPT MCK exploring the HVL album through an immersive 3D interactive web and desktop application.

## Product Purpose
Deliver a state-of-the-art interactive 3D gallery and audiophile-grade player experience for the HVL album, complete with synced lyrics, gapless playback, video visualizers, and tactile playback controls.

## Brand Personality
Cyber-minimalist, sleek obsidian dark theme, refined brutalist typography (Geist Mono), smooth micro-interactions, vinyl record aesthetics.

## Anti-references
Generic music players, cluttered widgets, distracting bright elements, stuttering audio transitions.

## Design Principles
1. Immersive 3D Gallery: 3D vinyl covers react naturally to scroll and mouse gestures.
2. Gapless & Tactile Audio: Instant transitions, responsive volume and seek controls with zero latency.
3. Clean Floating Dock: Essential controls neatly placed with clear visual hierarchy.
4. Cohesive Cyber Obsidian Theme: Dark glassmorphic materials with high contrast and crisp typography.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Full keyboard accessibility, standard ARIA labels on all audio and dock actions, support for reduced motion, high contrast on dark surfaces.
